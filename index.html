DarkTwist0 Publisher

DarkTwist0 Publisher is a personal tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @darktwist0 to prepare and publish their own original short videos (dark stories and quizzes) to their own TikTok account.

The creator reviews each video in a private dashboard, chooses the privacy level and the publishing time, and explicitly approves every post before it is sent to TikTok through the official Content Posting API.

The app is used only by its owner. It does not offer sign-up to other users and does not access any account other than the one the owner connects.
