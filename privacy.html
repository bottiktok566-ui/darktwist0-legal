Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Scope

DarkTwist0 Publisher ("the App") is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @darktwist0. It does not collect data from visitors or other users.

2. Data the App handles

  • TikTok authorization tokens of the owner's own account (access and refresh token), obtained through TikTok Login Kit with the scopes the owner grants.
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok for that account (such as display name), used to show which account is connected and which posting options are available.
  • Videos and captions created by the owner with the App, stored on the owner's own server until published or deleted.

3. How the data is used

Tokens are used only to publish the owner's approved videos to the owner's account. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for profiling.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on a private server controlled by the owner. The dashboard is password-protected. Tokens are kept in a file readable only by the server user.

5. Retention and deletion

The owner can delete any video from the dashboard and can delete the token file at any time. Access can also be revoked from TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

6. Third parties

To produce videos the App sends script text to a text-generation service and a speech service, and requests stock footage from a video provider. No TikTok account data is sent to them.

7. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/bottiktok566-ui/darktwist0-legal.
